===========

📦 #1: From Surplus to Social Good
How might we help large institutions identify, match, and move usable surplus - such as food, furniture, supplies, or equipment - to
community organizations before it becomes waste, so that fewer resources are discarded while more local needs are met?
Why this matters
Hospitals, campuses, retailers, and workplaces often discard usable items
because availability, storage, pickup logistics, and recipient needs are
managed separately. Meanwhile, community organizations may lack
visibility into resources that could support the people they serve.

Who could benefit
Sustainability, facilities, and operations teams

Community organizations and nonprofit partners

Food-recovery, reuse, and recycling networks

Individuals and communities served by local organizations

Challenge
Build a solution that helps organizations reduce waste by making surplus
visible, matching it to verified community needs, and simplifying the
handoff from donor to recipient. Teams may focus on food recovery, reuse
of equipment or furniture, circular supply chains, donation logistics,
impact reporting, or another waste-reduction opportunity. Teams should
go deep on a clearly defined waste stream, user need, and measurable
outcome.

OCI / Oracle opportunity
Use OCI Generative AI to organize and synthesize surplus-item information, help
match available resources with community needs, or create clear impact stories
from operational data. OCI can also support accessible experiences and scalable
coordination among participating organizations.

Responsible innovation
Use safe, appropriate data and design for transparency between participating
organizations. Teams should consider how their solution builds trust, verifies
information, and avoids creating unnecessary barriers for community partners.

What success looks like
A compelling waste-reduction or circularity problem with clear community
value

A usable prototype that makes surplus easier to identify, match, or redistribute

Measurable potential for waste avoided and resources redirected

A credible path for organizations and community partners to adopt the solution

Meaningful application of Oracle and OCI capabilities
