# MASTER SPECIFICATION: HAVENGRID (B2B / ORG-TO-ORG SURPLUS EXCHANGE)

You are an expert full-stack engineer and hackathon architect. Build a complete, production-polished web prototype called **HavenGrid** for the Oracle Hackathons International challenge ("From Surplus to Social Good").

## 1. PRODUCT FOCUS (STRICT ORG-TO-ORG SCOPE)
HavenGrid is strictly an **Organization-to-Organization (O2O)** platform connecting **Institutional Donors** (Universities, Corporate Campuses, Hospitals, Hotels, Conference Centers) with verified **Recipient Community Organizations** (Food Banks, Soup Kitchens, Shelters). 
*Do NOT include individual consumer apps or street kiosks in this build.*

The core workflow has 4 seamless stages:
1. **Event Registration & Pre-Event Leftover Prediction**: Donors register an event with start/end times, **RSVP Count**, and **Food Ordered (for how many people)**. AI predicts expected surplus before the event begins.
2. **T-Minus 45-Minute Automated Ping & AI Food Eval**: 45 minutes before the event ends, the organizer receives an automated prompt asking how much food is left over (e.g., 100 meal packets) and optionally uploads a photo for **OCI GenAI Vision** to tag allergens, shelf-life, and storage requirements.
3. **Nearby Food Bank Notification & Partial/Split Claiming**: Nearby verified Food Banks are notified immediately. If 100 meal packets are available:
   - A food bank can choose the exact quantity they want to claim (from 1 up to 100, capped by their available storage capacity).
   - If Food Bank A claims all 100, `remaining_quantity` becomes `0` and the batch immediately flips to `UNAVAILABLE / FULLY CLAIMED`.
   - If Food Bank A only claims 50, `remaining_quantity` updates live to `50`, and the remaining 50 stay `AVAILABLE` for other nearby food banks to claim until the counter hits `0`.
4. **Pickup Manifest & Impact Reporting**: Donors see a live breakdown of which food banks claimed which quantities, verify handoffs, and generate automated ESG / Good Samaritan tax deduction reports via **Oracle Autonomous Database Select AI**.

- **OCI Architecture & Database Schema**:
  - Service adapter in `/src/lib/oci-service.ts` supporting **OCI Generative AI (Vision & Text)** and **Oracle AI Database 26ai (Vector + Spatial SQL)** with instant local fallbacks for 100% reliable live demos.
  - Include `/db/schema_oracle26ai.sql` showing atomic inventory decrement locks (`UPDATE surplus_batches SET remaining_qty = remaining_qty - :claim_qty WHERE batch_id = :id AND remaining_qty >= :claim_qty`), spatial proximity (`SDO_WITHIN_DISTANCE`), and vector matching.
